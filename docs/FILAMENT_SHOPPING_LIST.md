# Colours and materials

Laurine has a cream deck, burgundy topsides, a dark blue-green bottom, varnished timber and stainless fittings. The seven colours below are the targets used in the print project.

![Deck, timber and separate dark windows](images/removable_details_assembled.png)

Use **1.75 mm PLA** throughout the printed assembly. Plain brown PLA represents the wood; silver PLA represents stainless steel. The separate windows are solid dark inserts, not transparent glazing.

## The seven colours

| Colour | Where it goes | Filament candidate from the project research |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm ivory** · `#E6DFC0` | Deck, cabin, cockpit, hatch and hidden pins | [Spectrum PLA Premium · Ivory Beige, RAL 1015](https://www.filimprimante3d.fr/products/fil-3d-spectrum-pla-1-75-mm-ivory-beige) |

| **Dark teal** · `#355C66` | Underwater hull, long keel and rudder | [colorFabb PLA High Speed Pro · Azure Blue](https://colorfabb.com/pla-high-speed-pro-azure-blue) |
| **Mahogany brown** · `#8C4825` | Tiller, handrails, washboards, trim and display cradles | [Polymaker Panchroma Matte · Wood Brown](https://www.3djake.fr/polymaker/panchroma-pla-matte-wood-brown) |

These are **visual candidates from the September 2026 research**, not confirmed matches to printed samples. Choose a convenient local supplier; the links identify products, not a promise of the lowest price or available stock. The target colours were estimated from photographs.

The underwater teal is the least certain match: Azure Blue may be too blue. Wood Brown may look lighter and warmer than varnished mahogany. Print small swatches before committing to the large hull parts. Paint or a tinted finish can refine the match; a clear gloss finish can help the dark inserts suggest glass.

You do not need to buy seven full kilograms solely for this model. The entire project is estimated at about **420 g**, including supports, purges and test pieces; the window plate uses only about **2.5 g**. Samples or existing spools may be sufficient for the small details. [Per-colour estimates](../reports/filament_usage.json) are available if you need the breakdown.

## Four spools at a time

There are seven project colours, but **no plate needs more than four at once**. Change the loaded spools between plates. Before each print, match the required project colours to the actual slots on your AMS Lite.

The supplied profiles are provisional Generic PLA settings. Set the profiles for your chosen materials, calibrate them and slice again. Check spool dimensions and the adapter requirements for your own feeder before ordering.

## Small items for assembly

| Item | Used for |
|---|---|
| 2 mm metal rod | Continuous mast core; start around 378 mm and trim after fitting |
| 1.5 mm metal rod | One-piece boom core; start around 120 mm and trim after fitting |
| 1 mm metal rod | Spreader; about 56 mm finished span |
| 0.8 mm metal pins | Rudder and stern fittings; cut to fit |
| Fine model thread | Shrouds, stays and running rigging, including two backstays |
| Glue suitable for PLA | Hull joint and separate fittings |
| Fine abrasive paper, a small file, filler and matching paint | Cleaning and hiding the hull/deck seam |
